Rather a dry month this March, with just one post.

Scott Alexander wrote an excellent and mind boggling [post](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/being-john-rawls) on the philosopher John Rawls. Inception level mind boggling, really. It talks about the philosophy of charity, being generous and explores the question - would you help someone when you can and how that affects whether someone would help you when you need.

A few interesting comments along the same line.
